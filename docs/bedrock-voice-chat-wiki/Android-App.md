From your Android phone or web browser use one the following links to get access to the open test on the Google Play Store

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alaydriem.bvc.client
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.alaydriem.bvc.client

The current build supports Android 16, 15, 14, 13, 12, and 11. Android 10 and below will not be supported.
