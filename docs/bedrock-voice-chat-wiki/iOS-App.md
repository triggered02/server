The iOS app will be released once we reach our membership and funding goal. Unlike the Play Store release which is a lifetime license fee (and just time) releasing the iOS app to the store undergoing the Apple review process and making any changes they require and maintaining that listing has real costs (developer fees, review changes, permanent demo services for reviewers, certificates, hosting of deep link functionality - just to name a few). This is hundreds of dollars a year just to keep the lights on before we even get into adding new features.

So while I want to make the iOS app (like the Android app) free for everyone, we need to meet our funding goals to ensure maintaining it.

Sponsors will receive access to the iOS Beta first once it is ready. You can help accelerate this process by supporting Bedrock Voice Chat. See the following forum post for more details on how you can do this: https://discord.com/channels/1158951318166183936/1466979335255752899
