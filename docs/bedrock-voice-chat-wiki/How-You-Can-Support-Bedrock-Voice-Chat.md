Bedrock Voice Chat has been a passion project from the start — born out of a love for Minecraft and the belief that players deserve a seamless, high-quality voice chat experience that brings worlds and friends closer together.

What started as an idea has grown into a full-fledged platform — one that's taken months of development, testing, editing, and investment. From studio-grade audio gear and mobile test devices to app store fees and endless hours of code, each step has been fueled by a single goal:

**to make real-time voice communication simple, reliable, and free for every Bedrock player, across platforms**

If you've enjoyed using Bedrock Voice Chat or believe in what it brings to the community, your support helps make it all possible. Financial contributions go directly toward maintaining the servers, improving the client, and continuing to release updates — always free and accessible to everyone.

## How You Can Support

I've set up a few ways for you to join the supporters who help keep Bedrock Voice Chat thriving:

1. Become a Channel Member or Twitch Subscriber
Join the community on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@alaydriem)
 or [Twitch](https://twitch.tv/alaydriem)
 — members get exclusive updates, early feature previews, and a front-row seat to development progress.

2. Back Me on Patreon
Visit [Patreon.com/Alaydriem](https://www.patreon.com/c/Alaydriem) and become a Bedrock Voice Chat Supporter. Every pledge directly funds ongoing development, app store maintenance, and future innovations.

## Why It Matters

Your support isn't just about funding — it's about keeping a community-driven project alive. It means more updates, faster features, and a stronger voice chat experience for everyone.

Together, we can make Bedrock Voice Chat not just a addon— but a cornerstone of how Minecraft Bedrock players connect.

## How you can support for free!

I understand not everyone can support financially.

1. Subscribe to my Youtube Channel: https://youtube.com/@alaydriem and watch the videos I produce (and comment and provide feedback). AdSense revenue is miniscule but every bit helps. All major announcements for Bedrock Voice Chat (along with other tools, information, and videos) will be shared here primarily as we announce things.

2. Follow me on Twitch: https://www.twitch.tv/alaydriem and watch the live streams! I stream twice weekly on a regular schedule. A larger twitch following and viewers helps raise ad revenue from Twitch that I re-invest back into making more videos and improving Bedrock Voice Chat.

3.  Share and use Bedrock Voice Chat, _and share your feedback_! Let your friends know about Bedrock Voice Chat, and report any issues or improvements that you'd like to see on Bedrock Voice Chat here on Github, or in my Discord

4. Help answer questions both on Github and Discord! See a question you know the answer to? Help answer them and help others solve issues they are having!
