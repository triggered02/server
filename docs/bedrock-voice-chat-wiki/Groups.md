For instances where you want to remain in constant communication with one or more players. Creating a new Group, or joining an existing group will ensure that all players in the group will be able to hear all players in that group at all times.

## Creating a new Group
New groups can be created by clicking the "New Group +" button in the left sub-sidebar menu. Once you create a group you are considered the group leader, and have the ability to either leave it to join another group, or close the group.

Leaving a group does not remove the creator as the owner, and the owner may rejoin at any time.

There is no restriction on the number of groups that may be created

## Managing a Group
The group creator can either leave or close the group they created. Closing a group will remove all members from that group, though it is recommended that players leave a group before it is closed

## Joining a Group

Joining a group will allow you to hear all conversations in that group. Your icon will change from Red to Purple in the sidebar once you join the group to help let you know what group you are currently in.

## Group Security
While all communication for BVC is fully end-to-end encrypted, any player in the group can hear you at any time, and may be recorded by other players.
