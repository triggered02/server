**NO**. At present, Bedrock Voice Chat does not work on Aternos Servers due to Aternos Servers not supporting upload-able behavior packs. The addon requires significant traction to before Aternos will consider it as a pre-installable mod on their servers.

_However_, you _may_ be able to embed the BVC behavior pack _into your world_ file. Assuming you can configure `@minecraft/server-net` into your variables.json, and embed the credentials into the pack itself.

I need to explore if this is possible, in which case BVC _might_ be installable as part of your world behavior pack on your custom Aternos server.

Aternos isn't a service I use, so if anyone of the community is interested in reviewing this _please_share your findings in https://discord.com/channels/1158951318166183936/1376229300033818684.
